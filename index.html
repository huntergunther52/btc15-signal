V2 automatically discovers the open KXBTC15M market and target. These fields remain as a fallback if the public API is unavailable in Safari.

How to read it

Avg needed is the average BTC price the remaining seconds would need for the full final-minute average to finish exactly at your target. During the last 60 seconds, the calculation progressively locks in observed one-second samples.

Important: V2 attempts Kalshi event live-data first. If Safari cannot consume that feed, the app explicitly labels Coinbase as INDICATIVE. Never treat an indicative fallback as the official settlement value.

Enter the exact target shown in the current Kalshi 15-minute BTC market. YES price is optional and is used only to calculate model-vs-market edge.

How to read it

Avg needed is the average BTC price the remaining seconds would need for the full final-minute average to finish exactly at your target. During the last 60 seconds, the calculation progressively locks in observed one-second samples.

Important: this MVP uses Coinbase BTC-USD as an indicative real-time feed. Kalshi states crypto contracts settle from the 60 one-second CF Benchmarks RTI observations in the expiration minute. Do not treat this app's displayed average as the official settlement value.
